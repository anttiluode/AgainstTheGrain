Drive the same route against the grain and the mirror starts to lie: surprise floods in, the accelerometer rings, and the clutch drops the neuron out of its cheap forward groove into the deliberate cognitive grid. The heart is the interference soma: a phasor settling toward a fixed point, firing when it lands inside its band. In habitual flow the mirror hands it the answer, so it holds still — cheap. Surprise kicks it off the fixed point and it has to spiral back, ringing the accelerometer with the f S S S … f f f motif. Enough sustained surprise and the clutch engages: the neuron drops the dissipative 1D groove for the reversible 2D grid, where any direction is equally cheap — but nothing is automatic. That handoff is the wrong turn in the park.

What it's actually computing

The same principle, doing arithmetic. An 8-bit adder whose habitual prediction for every bit is 0 — silence. A bit only wakes and spends its five gates when an input or an upstream carry violates that prediction. Small sums stay mostly dark. Big carries wake the whole ripple.
